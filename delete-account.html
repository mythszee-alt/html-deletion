Delete Your Account & Data

You can permanently delete your Faceva account and all associated data at any time. This page explains both ways to do it and exactly what gets removed.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app

The fastest way:

  1. Open Faceva
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can't open the app, send us a deletion request. Email us from the address linked to your Faceva account so we can verify it's you:

Email a deletion request

Or email mythszee@gmail.com manually with the subject "Delete My Account and Data" and include your account email address in the message.

What gets deleted

Data typeWhat happens
Photos and edited images stored on our serversPermanently deleted
Account details (email, display name)Permanently deleted
Usage and preference data linked to your accountPermanently deleted
Purchase and subscription recordsRetained only where required for tax, accounting, or legal obligations, then deleted
Anonymised, aggregated statisticsMay be kept — cannot be linked back to you

How long it takes

Your data is removed from our active systems immediately or within a few days of your request, and purged from encrypted backups within 30 days. We'll email you to confirm once your account has been deleted.

Good to know

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You'll need to create a new account to use Faceva again.
  • If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel the subscription separately in the Play Store → Subscriptions — deleting your account does not cancel billing.
  • Any images you already exported and saved to your own device or shared elsewhere are not affected and remain yours to manage.

For more detail on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
